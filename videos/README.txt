🎬 VIDEOS FOLDER 🎬
====================

Place your personal videos here!

RECOMMENDED:
- 2-5 short video clips
- Use MP4 format (most compatible)
- Keep each video under 50MB for best performance
- 10-30 seconds per clip is perfect!

NAMING TIPS:
------------
Use simple, descriptive names:
✓ beach-day.mp4
✓ birthday-2025.mp4
✓ funny-moment.mp4
✓ dance-together.mp4

AVOID:
✗ Special characters: #, @, %, &
✗ Spaces (use dashes instead)
✗ Very long names

VIDEO IDEAS:
------------
🎥 Candid moments
😂 Funny clips together
🎂 Birthday celebrations
🌊 Beach/vacation videos
💃 Dancing together
🎤 Singing/karaoke
🎁 Gift reactions
🚗 Road trip clips

HOW TO USE:
-----------
1. Put your videos in this folder
2. Open index.html
3. Find the videos gallery section
4. Replace video URLs with: videos/your-video-name.mp4

Example:
<video src="videos/beach-day.mp4" muted></video>

TIPS:
-----
✓ Keep videos short (10-30 seconds each)
✓ MP4 format works on all browsers
✓ Compress large videos online: https://www.freeconvert.com/video-compressor
✓ Videos will have a play button overlay

That's it! Your videos will appear in the gallery! 🎬✨
