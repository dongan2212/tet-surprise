📸 IMAGES FOLDER 📸
===================

Place your personal photos here!

RECOMMENDED:
- 5-10 photos of special moments
- Use JPG or PNG format
- Keep each photo under 5MB
- Landscape orientation works best (but portrait is fine too!)

NAMING TIPS:
------------
Use simple, descriptive names:
✓ first-date.jpg
✓ vacation-2025.jpg
✓ birthday-surprise.png
✓ us-at-beach.jpg

AVOID:
✗ Special characters: #, @, %, &
✗ Spaces (use dashes instead)
✗ Very long names

PHOTO IDEAS:
------------
🌸 First date photos
❤️ Candid happy moments
🌅 Sunset/sunrise together
✈️ Travel memories
🍜 Food dates
🎉 Celebrations
🌳 Nature walks
💑 Couple selfies

HOW TO USE:
-----------
1. Put your photos in this folder
2. Open index.html
3. Find the gallery section
4. Replace image URLs with: images/your-photo-name.jpg

Example:
<img src="images/first-date.jpg" alt="Our first date">

That's it! Your photos will appear in the gallery! ✨
